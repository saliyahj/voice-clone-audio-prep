# Workflow Notes

## Clip Selection Criteria

When selecting audio clips for voice clone testing, I would prioritize clips that are clear, consistent, and easy to understand.

## What Makes a Good Clip

- Minimal background noise
- One main speaker
- Clear pronunciation
- Natural speaking tone
- Enough length to capture voice characteristics

## What To Avoid

- Overlapping voices
- Loud music or background noise
- Distorted audio
- Extremely short clips
- Clips with inconsistent volume
